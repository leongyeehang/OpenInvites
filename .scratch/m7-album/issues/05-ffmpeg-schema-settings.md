# 05: Videos: ffmpeg in the image (ADR-0007), the video schema, and the event's video settings

**What to build:** The image carries ffmpeg and the app knows at start whether it is usable. The database learns what a video is: an item's kind, its length, its codec, whether a lighter copy exists, the states processing and failed, a job queue and a table of uploads in progress. The host's Album settings gain Allow videos, the two caps and the lighter copy's quality. No video is accepted yet; ticket 06 does that.

**Blocked by:** 04 Storage: files stream (the uploads route's lookup takes the video files)
