# 07: Videos in the album: the picker, the tiles and the player

**What to build:** Guests add videos from the album's picker. A clip over the event's caps is refused in the browser before a byte goes up; parts resume after a dropped connection. The album shows a Processing tile to the uploader and the hosts until the clip is ready, then a video tile with its length; a failed clip says so and can be removed. The viewer plays a video with the browser's own player, offers Original quality and Lighter copy when both exist, remembers the viewer's choice on the device, and offers Download of the original.

## Notes for the implementer

- Headless Chrome plays H.264 and AAC but not HEVC, which is what makes the HEVC row meaningful. A video's "Original quality" is the clip as the phone sent it: iOS compresses in the picker unless the guest chooses "Actual size", which is theirs.
- Commit subjects, e.g. `Album: videos from the picker, checked before sending and resumed after a drop`, `Album: video tiles, processing and failed`, `Album: the viewer plays a video and offers two qualities`.

How it landed:
- Ruling: the album routes' origin check applies to state-changing methods only. A browser sends no Origin with a same-origin GET, and the resume's GET of the parts received was refused, which the dropped-part row found; the API spec now pins the plain GET. Cost if wrong: none for GET, which answers only part numbers to the upload's owner.
- Ruling: the quality control sits under the player rather than in the viewer's header beside Download, where the header's row is already full at a phone's width. Cost if wrong: a control a thumb's length lower.
- The player's local-storage choice and the codec check go through `useSyncExternalStore` with server snapshots, not an effect that sets state, which the repository's lint rule refuses; the codec answer is remembered per codec.
- `chooseQuality`, `playable` and `missingParts` are unit-tested; the e2e proves the HEVC original is offered but disabled and the copy plays (`currentSrc`).
- Axe runs on the album with a video tile; the open viewer is not axe-checked here, since a guest's clip has no captions track and the rule for one cannot be satisfied by user content; ticket 11 decides whether to exclude that rule for the viewer.
- No reduced-motion row was added: the tiles and the player have no transition or pulse; ticket 11's reduced-motion pass can extend `expectStill` to them.

Things to know:
- The approval path for videos is the photos' (`photoStatusForOwner`); only photos are browser-tested under approval. The pending note and Approve appear under a video tile as they do under a photo's.
- A photo's per-file problems keep their old strings; a video's pre-send refusals name the limit and the file's length or size.
