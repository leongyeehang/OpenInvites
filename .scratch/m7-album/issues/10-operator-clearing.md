## Notes for the implementer

- Listing a large bucket in a request is acceptable for a settings page the operator presses a button on; a site with tens of thousands of files would wait a few seconds.
- Commit subjects, e.g. `Site settings: the operator clears an album, and its hosts are told`, `Site settings: the operator finds and removes stray files`.
