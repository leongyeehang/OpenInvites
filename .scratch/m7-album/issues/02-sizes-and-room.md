# 02: Storage: every upload records its size, and the album's room is counted in bytes

**What to build:** Every album photo and every host picture records its size as uploaded. An event's album is bounded by the items limit, Storage per event and Storage for the site; the host's poster upload is bounded by Storage for the site; on local disk every upload stops when under 1 GB is free. Refusals name the limit in plain units. Files already on the site are measured once in the background after the upgrade.

## Notes for the implementer

- The event-row lock serialises one album's uploads; it does not serialise the site's sum across events, so two albums uploading at once can overshoot Storage for the site by one post each. Accepted; ticket 11's docs say so (Review Focus 7).
- `countPhotos` (`src/album/repository.ts:37-43`) counts every row; ticket 05 excludes failed videos once that status exists.
- Commit subject, e.g. `Storage: uploads record their size, and albums are bounded in bytes`.
