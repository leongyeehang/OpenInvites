# 01: Site settings: every limit on the Limits card, in effect at once

**What to build:** The operator sets the largest photo, the largest video, the longest video, the storage per event, the storage for the site and the default album reminder on the Limits card of Site settings; every value counts from the moment it is saved. `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` retires: its value is copied into Largest photo at the first start after the upgrade, and the environment setting is ignored from then on. A pure module formats sizes and lengths for people.

**Blocked by:** nothing
