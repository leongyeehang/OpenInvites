# 09: Album: the host empties the album, and is reminded about it

**What to build:** A host empties an event's album in one confirmed step. A host picks when to be reminded about the album after the event; on that day the hosts are emailed and the manage page and dashboard show a notice with Empty the album and Keep it. Nothing is deleted on a timer.

**Blocked by:** 06 Videos: the upload protocol, the queue and the processing (items and uploads in progress exist)
