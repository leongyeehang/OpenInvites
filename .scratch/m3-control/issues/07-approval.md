# 07: Approval

**What to build:** With approval on, a reply waits for the host. Pending and declined guests see the invitation but not the guest list or the comments. The host approves or declines from the guest list; approval applies the capacity rule and tells the guest by email.

**Blocked by:** 06 Capacity and the waitlist

**Status:** done

- [x] Schema: enum `rsvp_approval` (`approved`, `pending`, `declined`); `rsvp.approval rsvp_approval not null default 'approved'`; `event.approval_required boolean not null default false`. Migration 0030.
- [x] `admit` (`src/rsvps/admission.ts`) honours `approvalRequired` and `invited`: a reply with no `current` on an event requiring approval is `pending` with the status it asked for, unless `invited`; a current `pending` reply stays `pending` on edit; a current `declined` reply stays `declined` on edit; an approved reply is admitted as task 6 does. A pending or declined reply never takes space.
- [x] Approve and decline (`approveGuestAction`, `declineGuestAction` in `src/rsvps/actions.ts`, gate `can(role, "guests")`), in the transaction: approve sets `approved` and admits the reply's own status with `approvalRequired: false` (Going fits or is waitlisted), promotes the waitlist, and queues one mail when the guest gave an email: `Mail.approved.{subject,body}` "You're in: {title}" with their reply, the event link and the footer, or `Mail.approvedWaitlisted` saying they are on the waitlist at their position; decline sets `declined` and sends nothing. Turning approval off in the event form approves every pending reply through the same path (each gets the mail); turning it on changes no reply.
- [x] Visibility: `guestListView(visibility, { hasRsvp })` and `commentsView`, `takesComments` and `commenter` count only an approved reply; a pending guest's locked line is "Once the host approves your reply you'll see who's coming." (`EventPage.lockedPending`) and a declined guest's is "The host wasn't able to accept your reply."; the public guest list, the public counts and the Thread header exclude pending and declined replies; `RsvpCounts` gains `pending`.
- [x] Guest-facing, every layout: the confirmation for a pending reply "Your reply is waiting for the host's approval." (Thread: "Thanks, {name}! I'll check with the host and let you know."), for a declined reply the line above, each with the usual edit and withdraw actions. `GuestRsvp` gains `approval`.
- [x] Host guest list: **Waiting for approval** at the top with Approve and Decline on each row, **Declined** at the bottom with Approve; the headline adds "{count} waiting for approval" when any; the dashboard card says "{count} waiting for approval". `groupByStatus` orders: Waiting for approval, Going, Waitlist, Maybe, Can't go, Declined.
- [x] Mail: `recipients` takes only approved replies (announcements, reminders, promotion); `cancellationAudience` keeps Going, Maybe and Waitlist replies whether approved or pending; `notifyHostsOfRsvp` gets a notice `pending` with `Mail.rsvpReply.{subject,body}.pending` "{name} is waiting for your approval", with the guest list link.
- [x] Event form, Guests group: "Approve each guest before they're on the list" (`approvalRequired`), hint "Guests see the invitation while they wait, but not who's coming or the comments." Duplicate copies it.
- [x] Unit tests: `admit` with approval and `invited`; `guestListView` and `commentsView` with each approval; `recipients` filtering.
- [x] Browser tests (`e2e/approval.spec.ts`): the host turns on approval; a guest replies Going and sees the waiting line, the guest list locked with the pending line, and no comment form; the host sees them under Waiting for approval, approves, the guest's page on return shows Going with the list open, and Mailpit has "You're in" with the event's path; a second guest is declined, sees the line, and a comment post replayed from their browser context (caught with `page.route` as `co-hosts.spec.ts` replays a delete) is refused with no comment appearing; the host approves them later; with capacity 1, approving a second Going guest lands them on the waitlist with the waitlist mail; turning approval off approves the pending guest that remains.

## Notes for the implementer

- `comments/actions.ts` `commenter(eventId)` and `page.tsx`'s `viewer` are the two places that decide who is a guest with a reply; both read the approval.
- Commit subject, e.g. `Approval: a host can approve each reply before the guest is on the list`.

Decisions and things to know:
- A pending or declined guest sees the guest list and the comments exactly as a visitor does (the dispatch's ruling): under "Always" the list is open to them, as to anyone with the link; under "After you reply" it is locked with their own line; the comments are always locked for them. The spec's "not the guest list … which are locked for them" read literally would hide an "Always" list from a waiting guest that any visitor sees; I followed the ruling.
- The comments' locked line for a guest who has replied could not be the visitor's "Reply to join the conversation", so two keys the ticket does not name: `comments.lockedPending` "Once the host approves your reply you can join the conversation." and `comments.lockedDeclined` (the declined line).
- The approval mail goes to every approved guest who gave an email, Can't go included, as the ticket says ("You’re in: {title}" … "You said Can’t go."). A host approving a Can't go reply is rare and the mail is accurate about the reply, but "You’re in" reads oddly for it; for the maintainer.
- The hosts' notice reads "{name} replied to {title} and is waiting for your approval" (subject) and "{name} replied to {title}: {status}…, and is waiting for your approval." then "Approve or decline the reply on your guest list:" and the link: the ticket's words with the event named, as task 6 did for the waitlist notice. Approving and declining tell no host (the ticket: approve queues one mail, the guest's; decline sends nothing). A pending or declined reply that changes its status tells the hosts "changed", and its removal "removed", as any reply's.
- The calendar is not offered on a pending or declined confirmation (`offersCalendar`): the guest is not on the list. The Broadsheet stamp stays "Received" for both; the heading says how it stands.
- Approving sets `updated_at`, so the row shows "Changed …" afterwards, as after Move to Going.
- `RsvpCounts.declined` is beyond the ticket: without it the delete warning ("N RSVPs … are deleted") would undercount. `PublicGuest` carries `approval` (always approved there) so the public list counts through the same `asTally`.
- A guest declined, who withdraws and replies again, is pending again (a new RSVP; covered by the Broadsheet test). Approval cannot be taken back from an approved reply (no Decline on it), so a guest with comments is always approved; `mayDelete` is unchanged.
- The replies-per-event limit counts pending and declined replies (it counts rows).
- The upgrade: the dev database took 0032 at the standalone server's start (every existing RSVP approved, every event without approval); `db-fresh` and `db-no-mail` took it on the rebuilt containers' start (33 migrations each). `drizzle-kit check` reports no drift.
