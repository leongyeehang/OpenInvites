# 10: Retention

**What to build:** Events are deleted, with everything about them, a set time after they end, a year by default; the hosts are warned a month before by email and on the event's page; nothing is deleted sooner than a month after the warning. The worker becomes the instance's clock and runs with or without mail.

**Blocked by:** 03 The custom link (the manage link in the warning), 08 Personal invite links (the tables that cascade)

**Status:** done

## Notes for the implementer
