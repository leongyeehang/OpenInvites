# 29: Sections: a host adds Notes and Links to the page

**What to build:** "Add a section" at the foot of Arrange offers a Note and Links, with suggestions that fill in the heading: Dress code, Getting there and Gifts for a Note, Registry and Playlist for Links. A new section lands after the open row, or at the end, and its own sheet opens over the live copy: a Note is written in the About editor; Links are rows of a label and an address, added, removed and moved by buttons. Guests see Notes and Links in every layout; a link opens in a new tab without a referrer and shows its site's name under the label. In the editor they are drawn in full and open their sheets. A Note or Links is removed from its row after a confirmation. They belong to the page alone: never in its metadata, preview card, calendar file or any email, nor on the locked page. A duplicated event keeps them.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** A link with no label shows its site's name as the label; an address typed without a scheme is saved with `https://` (ticket 24's `safeLinkUrl`).
- Nothing is fetched for a link: no icon, no preview, no redirect through the site (ADR-0005). `nofollow` is there because event pages are never indexed anyway (ADR-0004) and a host's link is not the site's endorsement.
- A Note's rich text may hold links as the description's does (`safeHref`: http, https, mailto); a Links section's are http and https only, checked on save and again by `linksToDraw` on drawing.
- The metadata, the preview card (`src/app/e/[slug]/preview.png`), the calendar file and the mail read the event's own fields, never `sections`: the test proves it stays so.
- The ten-note row is slow by hand; add the ten through the sheet's button in a loop, waiting on each row to appear.
