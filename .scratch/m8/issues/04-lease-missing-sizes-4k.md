# 04: Videos: leases are renewed, the operator makes missing sizes, and may have a 4K copy made

**What to build:** Three things for the operator's side of processing. A job's lease is ten minutes, renewed every two while it runs, and a renewal that finds the job gone stops ffmpeg (M7's deferred minor M5). A Make missing sizes button on the Storage card queues every ready video that lacks a size its event asks for, so videos added before 0.8 get the new sizes without leaving their albums. And a site switch, off by default, "Also make a 4K copy that plays everywhere": when on, a 4K original that is not H.264 also gets a 4K H.264 copy, `video-2160p.mp4`, which the player lists as "4K".

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The 4K copy goes only to an original whose short side is 2160 or more and whose codec is not H.264: an H.264 4K original already plays everywhere. It is never the copy made before ready, since a 4K encode on a small server takes far longer than the 1080p one the item needs to play.
- **(plan)** Make missing sizes is offered only while "Process videos for playback" is on and ffmpeg is usable, and the card counts what it would queue before the press, so the operator sees what one click starts. The count is O(videos), read when Site settings renders.
- **(plan)** A copy's timeout grows with the clip: 30 times its length for 4K and 8 times for the rest, never under an hour. A long 4K encode is no longer cut off, and renewal keeps its lease.
- The lease is renewed from the app's clock, as it is claimed, so `claimable` compares like with like.
- The 4K switch counts for what is processed afterwards; Make missing sizes brings older 4K clips up to it.
