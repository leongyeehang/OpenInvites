# 14: Voting: the host says who may vote and when voting closes, and tells guests it is open

**What to build:** The host says who may vote: everyone who sees the whole album (the default), only guests who are going, or only guests who replied through a personal invite link; hosts always may. The host may set a time at which voting closes by itself; guests see it beside "Voting is open", and once it has passed, pages and the vote action treat voting as closed, with no timer running. Starting a vote offers an announcement already written, "Tell guests voting is open", which opens the Announcements page with the message filled in for the host to edit and send. Nothing is sent by itself.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** "Only guests invited personally" is an RSVP tied to a personal invite (`rsvp.invite_id` is set): replied through its link, or on the list of an invite-only event, where `invitesNewReply` ties every reply. A host who deletes the invite unties the reply, and that guest may no longer vote.
- **(plan)** Changing who may vote keeps the votes already given and governs who votes from then on; the hint says so. Taking votes away from a guest who changed their reply would make a vote silently vanish.
- **(plan)** A draft reaches the Announcements page as `?draft=voting` (and ticket 16's `?draft=winners`): the page asks `votingDraft` and starts its form with the text and, when only guests who are going may vote, with Going alone ticked. "Invited personally" has no status to tick, so the form's usual audience stays. The host edits it and presses Send; nothing goes otherwise. The draft is written in the host's language, as "Send a reminder now" is.
- **(plan)** The closing time is entered and shown in the event's own zone, as the guest list writes times; a time that has passed is refused when it is set, and a vote that reaches the time closes without anything running.
- The test that waits for the closing time waits on the page's state, reloading, never on a fixed sleep; `page.clock` is not used.
