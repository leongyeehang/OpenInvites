# 09: Album: whoever adds an item may caption it, and the album's media are reviewed in every language

**What to build:** Whoever adds a photo or video, and any host, may give it a caption of up to 140 characters of plain text, while adding it or afterwards, and change or remove it later. The caption is shown under the item in the viewer and is part of the item's accessible name in the grid and the viewer. Changing one counts against the comment limit. Then the media part's pass: every string tickets 02 to 09 added read in both Chinese scripts, axe, keyboard and reduced motion over every new state, a zh-Hant row, the operator docs, a note on ADR-0007, and the start of the 0.8 upgrade note, which says the app and the worker container are updated together.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The caption is shown under the item in the viewer and is in the item's accessible name everywhere; the grid draws no caption under its tiles, which at a third of a phone's width would hold four words.
- **(plan)** "While adding" is a field on each added file's status line: the picker sends files as soon as they are picked, and a step before sending would change the add flow every guest knows.
- **(plan)** Line breaks become spaces and the length is counted by code point, as Postgres counts it; an empty caption removes it. Plain text: React escapes it, nothing is parsed.
- Anything tickets 02 to 08 left as a follow-up in their Comments is listed here for the maintainer, not fixed silently (M7 ticket 11's rule).
- A caption is the uploader's words, not a detail of the file: the host's detail switches do not hide it.
