# 06: Album: a video plays at the size the viewer picks, or at Auto's, which steps down when it stalls

**What to build:** The viewer's player offers a video's sizes by name in one radio group: Auto, the original under its real size with "Original" after it (and an HDR badge when its transfer is HLG or PQ), then the copies, largest first. Auto picks once, when the video opens, for the screen and the connection, and steps down one size with a short reload at the same position when playback stalls three times in a minute or once for six seconds; it never steps up within a video. A size the guest picks is remembered on the device under `openinvites.videoSize`; M7's `openinvites.videoQuality` is read once. An original this browser cannot decode is offered disabled and explained, so a guest whose browser cannot play an iPhone's 4K is told and watches a copy. Download stays the original.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** "The original, whatever its size" is `{ side: Infinity, original: true }`, stored as "original": M7's "original" names no size, and on a 4K HEVC original this browser cannot decode it gives the 4K copy, the nearest thing.
- **(plan)** Stepping down goes to the largest size with a smaller side: an original that stalls at 2160 goes to 1080p, since a 4K copy of the same side would stall as well.
- **(plan)** Auto's size is fixed for the open video; choosing Auto again keeps it. A `waiting` caused by a seek is not a stall.
- `playsInline`, `preload="metadata"`, the poster and the resume stay as M7 built them (`video-player.tsx:87-106`). No player library (spec, Constraints).
- `screen` in Playwright equals the viewport unless set, which makes Auto's answers in the browser tests above exact: desktop 1280x800 and mobile 390x844, both at ratio 1.
- Ticket 11 makes `name` nullable: keep the player's `label` a prop built by the caller.
- In code and messages the word is "size", never quality or resolution (plan, Global Constraints); `copyName` and `VIDEO_QUALITIES` are ticket 02's to rename or keep.
