# 30: Sections: the host chooses how the album, the comments and Who's coming are drawn

**What to build:** A row in Arrange that offers styles opens to them as large visual swatches, each a small drawing of the style with its name. The Album is drawn as a grid (today's), a mosaic (the first item large) or a strip (one row, swiped sideways). The Comments are a list (today's) or chat bubbles. Who's coming is names (each layout's drawing of today) or avatars (a circle of initials over each first name). ## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The first style of each kind is what every layout draws today (grid, list, names), so a page never styled is unchanged; the others are one drawing each, shared by the three layouts, framed by each layout.
- **(plan)** A section locked to the viewer is drawn as today whatever its style: a style shows what is there, and a locked list shows nothing.
- The strip is swiped by the browser's own scrolling; the viewer's swipe handlers (`album.tsx:306,329-337`) belong to the open viewer and do not apply to the strip. A tile that takes the focus is scrolled into view by the browser.
- The test profile takes three upload asks a minute per address: add the three photos in one batch.
- If ticket 08, 09 or the voting tickets have not landed when this one starts, the rows that need them wait for them; nothing here builds their parts.

- **(plan)** The voting control on each album item is ticket 12's `AlbumSlot` (`{ head; tile; viewer; lists }`, a prop of `Album` in `src/themes/sections/album.tsx`): `slot.tile[id]` holds the star, the count and the ribbon (`data-slot="vote-star"`, `"vote-count"`, `"vote-ribbon"`), `slot.head` the award picker and the Winners strip, and thumbnails carry the id `album-item-{id}` that the star's accessible name points at. Every album style draws `slot.tile[id]` on its tile and keeps that id, and `slot.lists` still decides which items are listed. Where ticket 15 has not landed, nothing here changes.
