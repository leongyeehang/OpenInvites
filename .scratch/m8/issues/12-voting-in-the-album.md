# 12: Voting: guests and hosts vote in the album, with counts and ribbons once it closes

**What to build:** While the album vote is open, every item a person may vote for wears a star. One tap gives that person's vote in the award, a tap on another item moves it, a tap on the voted item takes it back. Nobody votes for their own upload, and only approved items take votes. Guests see no counts while voting is open; hosts see them live, with Close voting and Reopen voting in a box only hosts see. Once voting closes, every item shows its count and the top three wear ribbons, with shared places on ties. The vote is checked again on the server, under the event's lock, with a rate limit of its own. This ticket has one award, the Favourite; ticket 13 adds more.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The chosen award lives in the page's query as `?award=<id>`, set with `window.history.replaceState` (ticket 13's picker) and read with `useSearchParams` by the album and the module's components (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/01-getting-started/04-linking-and-navigating.md`, "Native History API"). Switching costs no request, so it spends nothing of the event page limit a party shares; the server works out every award's list once; and an announcement can link to one award.
- **(plan)** A star names its item through the thumbnail's `id` and `aria-labelledby`, because a slot is built on the server before the album numbers its items, and the number depends on the award chosen.
- Ribbons are drawn once voting has closed, never while it is open, even for hosts.
- The album knows nothing of awards: the slot hands it nodes and lists of ids. Ticket 30's album styles draw the slot the same way in each style.
- The star stays 36px in the Thread's narrow column; a tile there is about 80px.
