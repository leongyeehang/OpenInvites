# 26: Sections: Comments, Who's coming, the Album and the Letter are switched on and off in Arrange

**What to build:** One switch, one truth. The Comments switch and the guest list's "who sees it" leave the Settings sheet for Arrange, where Who's coming has an On switch and, once a row opens, who sees it: everyone with the link or guests who have replied. "Only the hosts" becomes Who's coming switched off. The Album gains a switch of its own: with it off, guests see no album and its upload routes refuse, and nothing in it is deleted. The Letter's switch in Arrange is the same as the Invites page's. Where the site has the Album off, Arrange does not offer it. Each switch is a column, written in the same transaction as the arrangement.
