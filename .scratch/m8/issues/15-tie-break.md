# 15: Voting: a tie for first place can go to a tie-break among the tied items

**What to build:** Once voting has closed, an award whose first place is shared offers its hosts a tie-break: a new round among the tied items only, a fresh vote each, counts hidden from guests. Closing it puts the tied items in order among themselves by that round's votes, in the places they shared, so two items that shared first become first and second; every other item keeps its place from the first round. If the top is still level, the host chooses another round among those still level, or keeps the shared first place. A tie-break is offered for first place only, award by award.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** Names stay as the event's setting has them once voting has closed: during a tie-break `namesShown` reads the event's state, closed, so under "Shown after voting closes" names remain. Hiding again what every guest has already seen would protect nothing.
- **(plan)** A tile's count stays the first round's after a tie-break and the tie-break's votes are said beside it; the ribbons come from `awardPlaces`. Two tiles that both say 12 and wear 1st and 2nd are explained by "5 votes in the tie-break" and "3 votes in the tie-break".
- **(plan)** A tie-break has no closing time: the host closes it. The vote's closing time is for the first round, which has already closed.
- Each award's tie-break is its own. Starting one leaves the other awards' results as they are.
- This ticket may slip to a later release: ticket 16 draws only on ticket 10's rules, which already place the items of any closed tie-break, so nothing in 16 waits for this.
