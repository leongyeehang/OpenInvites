# 16: Voting: the winners head the album, guests vote in an order of their own, and the vote is reviewed in every language

**What to build:** Once voting closes, a Winners strip heads the album: for the chosen award, its first to third places with their pictures, ribbons, counts and, where guests may see them, who added them. The host is offered the winners as an announcement already written, to edit and send from the Announcements page. While voting is open, each guest sees the album in an order shuffled for them alone and stable across visits, so an early upload has no advantage; hosts see the usual order. Then the voting part's pass: every string read in both Chinese scripts, axe, keyboard, reduced motion and a locale row over every new state, the docs, the release note and the glossary proposals.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The shuffle is proven by unit tests: with a handful of items two guests' orders coincide by chance often enough to make a browser assertion that they differ a flake, and CI fails on any flake. The browser checks what cannot be chance: the order holds across visits, and hosts keep the usual one.
- **(plan)** The winners announcement names items by kind and uploader, not by number: a number depends on the list a viewer sees, and the message outlives the vote. Under Never shown to guests it names no one.
- **(plan)** The strip's pictures and names are handed to the hook by the album, which builds their URLs; the module still imports nothing of the album's. The hook drops a name `showsName` withholds before anything is sent.
- The shuffle is keyed by the guest's RSVP, so it is the same on every device that holds that reply, and an item added later takes its place without moving the others.
- Numbers in labels follow the list shown, shuffled or not; they never reveal the order of uploading.

Glossary proposals for CONTEXT.md (not edited):
- **Album vote**: a vote the hosts run on an event's album, in one or more awards, in which each person who may vote has one vote per award; started, closed, reopened and turned off from the Voting block. Not the date poll, whose "vote" and "voter" keep their own meaning. 相册投票 / 相簿投票.
- **Award**: one thing the album vote asks (Favourite, Best video), named by the host, for photos, videos or both; at most eight an event. Its results are its places and, for the first three, ribbons and the Winners strip. 奖项 / 獎項.
- **Tie-break**: another round of one award's vote, among the items sharing its first place, started and closed by the hosts once voting has closed, with its counts hidden from guests until it closes; a further round is "another round", and the hosts may instead keep first place shared. 决胜投票 / 決勝投票.
