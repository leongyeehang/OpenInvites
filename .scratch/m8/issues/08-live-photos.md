# 08: Album: an Android motion photo is added as a live photo that plays when pressed

**What to build:** A photo whose JPEG carries a motion clip (Google's motion photo, or Samsung's) is added as a live photo: it is a photo everywhere, in counts, approval and photo awards, and keeps a few seconds of its motion as a small silent H.264 MP4 beside its other files. Its tile and the viewer wear a Live mark; in the viewer the motion plays, muted, while the picture is held, or once when the mark, a button, is pressed; never by itself, and under reduced motion only from the button. The clip is taken out while the photo is finished and made by the video queue, so the photo is in the album at once as its still and gains its motion seconds later. Without ffmpeg, or with the site's Videos switched off, a live photo is its still. The add-photos screen tells a guest on an iPhone that a Live Photo is added as a photo, and that Save as Video adds its motion as a video.

## Notes for the implementer

- **(plan)** The motion is made by the video queue as a job of its own, not inline in the finish: photos are processed without the queue, but ffmpeg then runs where the operator put it (`VIDEO_PROCESSING=worker` keeps it off the app container), one encode at a time and at low priority, with the queue's tries and lease. The photo is ready at once as its still; the page refreshes while its motion is made, as it does for a processing video.
- **(plan)** The motion is kept while the site's Videos feature is on and ffmpeg is usable; an event's Allow videos does not stop it, since a live photo is a photo. A motion already made keeps playing after Videos is switched off, as videos do.
- **(plan)** On the tile the Live mark is a pill inside the tile's button, since a button inside a button is invalid; in the viewer the mark is the button and the picture plays while held.
- **(plan)** The iPhone note goes to iPhones and iPads only, judged from the user agent after hydration; where the event takes no videos it does not suggest adding one.
- **(plan)** "At most a few seconds" is three, about what a phone records around the shot; anything longer is cut. No sound (spec, Out of Scope).
- `motion-sent` is the clip as sent, never served, removed once the motion is made or given up; the stored still is re-encoded by sharp, which never reads past the JPEG's end, so the downloaded photo carries no motion and no metadata.
- iPhone Live Photos stay stills (maintainer, 2026-11-11): nothing joins a still to a saved video.

How it landed:
- Ruling: `MOTION_MAX_BYTES` is 32 MB, a cap on the clip's bytes beside the ticket's three seconds, so a range larger than any phone's few seconds is a file that lies and nothing of it is stored or handed to ffmpeg. Cost if wrong: a real clip over 32 MB stays a still.
- Ruling: retries. A `motion` try that throws before its last gives its lease back with `retryJob` (tries kept, `last_error` written, only while this processor holds it); the last, or a crashed-out claim, calls `giveUpMotion` (job and `motion-sent` go) with one log line. A clip ffprobe cannot read, or a `motion-sent` that is gone, is given up at once. `failJob` is never called for a `motion` job, and `JobGone` calls neither (the photo's files go only if the photo went, `removeItemFiles`).
- Ruling: the viewer's picture prevents the context menu while the hold is on (not under reduced motion), since Android Chrome's long-press menu would otherwise cancel the hold; Download is in the header.
- Ruling: the motion `<video>` is `aria-hidden` and `pointer-events-none` (the still beside it has the alt text; a swipe that starts on the playing motion still turns the page), hidden by `invisible` until its `playing` event so no blank frame shows, and loops while held, so a hold longer than the clip plays until let go.
- The picture's area becomes a size container for a live photo only, and the still is sized by `100cqh`/`100cqw`, so the wrapper is the picture and the Live mark sits on its top-left corner, not the letterbox's (checked by screenshot at both widths: picture, still and motion share one box).
