# 05: Videos: HDR copies come out in standard colour, and the original keeps its HDR

**What to build:** An original whose transfer is HLG or PQ is HDR. Its copies, and its poster and thumbnail, are tone-mapped to standard colour (BT.709) with ffmpeg's `zscale` and `tonemap` filters, so a guest on any screen sees natural colour; the original keeps its HDR. The start-up check learns whether this server's ffmpeg has those filters: without them, copies are made as in 0.7 and the Storage card says so. Every video records its HDR kind and frame rate in a new `details` column, which the player (ticket 06) and the technical line read. A checked-in HDR clip proves the conversion; what it cannot prove about an iPhone's Dolby Vision is said plainly below.

**Blocked by:** 04 Videos: leases are renewed, the operator makes missing sizes, and may have a 4K copy made

## Notes for the implementer

- **What the fixture proves, and what it cannot.** `clip-hdr.mp4` proves that an HLG original is recognised, keeps its HLG tags through the rewrap, and gets H.264 copies tagged BT.709 through the tone-mapping chain, and that processing writes `hdr` and `fps` (seen on the page from ticket 06's HDR mark). It cannot prove anything about Dolby Vision: no encoder in the image writes a Dolby Vision record (x265 needs a record taken from a real Dolby Vision source), so nothing here can make a clip that carries one. Whether the rewrap keeps an iPhone's record, and whether Safari still shows the original as Dolby Vision, needs a real iPhone clip. The rewrap passes `-strict unofficial` when the probe reports the record, since some ffmpeg versions write the MP4's `dvcC` box only under it; the unit test proves the flag is passed, not that the box survives **(plan)**.
- **The maintainer's check, for the release note.** Record an HDR clip on an iPhone (Settings, Camera, Record Video, HDR Video on), add it to an album on a test site, and run `docker compose exec app ffprobe -v error -show_streams -select_streams v:0 /app/uploads/<item id>-video.mp4`: a `DOVI configuration record` among its side data means the record survived. The docs line then becomes "kept, checked with ffmpeg 8.1.3 on <date>"; if it is missing, they say the original is kept as HLG HDR without its Dolby Vision record. The Comments entry says which check is still open.
- **(plan)** The poster and thumbnail of an HDR clip are tone-mapped too: they are taken from the original, and an untone-mapped frame looks as washed out as 0.7's copies did.
- **(plan)** `zscale` and `tonemap` were chosen over `libplacebo`, which wants a GPU, and `colorspace`, which does not tone-map. `npl=100` and `hable` are the common recipe; the maintainer judges the look by eye.
- If ffmpeg refuses a chain as written, find the one that works in the throwaway container, change `toneMapFilter` and its test to it, and say so in the Comments entry.
- No HDR copy is made (the spec's out of scope); the original is the HDR size.
