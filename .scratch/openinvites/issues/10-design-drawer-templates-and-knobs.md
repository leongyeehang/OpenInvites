# 10: Design drawer: templates and knobs

**What to build:** A host opens a Design drawer on their event, picks one of six templates, and adjusts background, title font, accent colour, text tone, and RSVP button style while the page updates live. Once anything changes, the theme reads "custom, started from" the template.

**Blocked by:** 07 Guest RSVP flow

**Status:** done

Supper club and Kids' party name the Broadsheet and Thread layouts; in M1 they apply with the Poster layout and the Layout row shows those two as "coming soon".
- [x] Drawer order: an Upload button first (a placeholder until ticket 12), then Templates with thumbnails, Layout, Background gallery, then a collapsed Details group holding title font, accent (auto plus six swatches), text tone (auto, light, dark), RSVP buttons (glass, solid accent, outline). ## Comments

**2026-09-23, implementing agent.** Done. Lint, type check, 178 unit tests, and 128 browser tests (64 scenarios at 390px and desktop) pass against the production image on the Compose `test` profile. The designer pass looked at all six templates in both text tones with all three button styles at 390px and 1280px, and a contrast audit measured 4,830 text elements on those renders against the pixels actually behind them: every one reads at WCAG AA.

How it landed:

- **Templates are data files** in `src/themes/templates/`, one per template (`birthday.ts`, `vows.ts`, `supper-club.ts`, `kids-party.ts`, `festival.ts`, `quiet.ts`), listed in drawer order in `index.ts`, shaped by `template.ts`, documented for contributors in `README.md` there. A template sets every knob except the host's own upload. `DEFAULT_THEME` is now `applyTemplate(null, birthday)`, so new events take their defaults from the Birthday file. Birthday and Festival carry round one's A and B knob for knob.
- **Pure rules** in `src/themes/theme.ts` (`applyTemplate`, `OFFERED_LAYOUTS`) and `src/themes/changes.ts` (`applyChange`, `themeReadout`, `parseThemeChange`). Applying a template copies its knobs and records `{ id, dirty: false }`; Supper club and Kids' party keep `broadsheet`/`thread` in their files and apply as `poster`. Any knob change marks the template dirty; choosing what is already chosen changes nothing, dirty flag included; re-applying a template resets. A new background puts the accent back on auto (the prototype's rule).
- **The drawer** is a Design button on the event page, rendered only for the event's host (`src/app/e/[slug]/design-drawer.tsx`), which opens a plain non-modal panel (`design-panel.tsx`, loaded only when first opened, so guests never download it). Order: Upload (a disabled placeholder saying "Coming soon"), Templates with thumbnails of the event's own title in each template's theme, Layout (Poster; Broadsheet and Thread disabled, "Coming soon"), Background gallery, then Details, collapsed, with title font, accent (Auto and six named swatches in `src/themes/swatches.ts`), text tone and RSVP buttons. The readout says "Theme: Birthday" or "Theme: Custom, started from Birthday". Escape closes it and focus returns to the Design button.
- **Live preview and saving: save on every change.** `ThemedPage` is now a client component holding the theme in `useOptimistic`; a change shows at once and `changeThemeAction` (`src/themes/actions.ts`) saves it, then `refresh()`es the page so the host settles on what guests get. The title (`poster-title.tsx`) and the RSVP buttons read the live theme from `useTheme()`. The page still renders on the server, so first paint is the finished look. The save is `changeEventTheme` in the events repository: read and write under a row lock, and every write moves `updatedAt`, so the preview card redraws (ticket 14's note). The status line in the panel says Saving…, Saved, or that a change did not save.
- **Nothing unreadable: `src/themes/legibility.ts`**, a pure rule with WCAG contrast maths. From each background's measured lightest and darkest points (new fields in `backgrounds.ts`, measured on the page's own backdrop with the blobs, scrim and fade, under the glass's 24px blur, at five viewport sizes, with the blobs at rest and at the far end of their drift), the text tone and the accent, it works out: the tint under the glass's frost (as little as reading needs: a light haze on the darker backgrounds, a smoked pane on the brightest), a veil behind text set straight on the page (outline buttons, the "not taking RSVPs" note), how far muted and faint text may fade, the text on the accent (plum or white, else black or white, which always reaches AA), and the accent's colour where it is set as text. Every colour is now an inline `--theme-*` variable from `themeVariables`; the per-tone blocks in `globals.css` are gone. Unit tests check every background, in both tones, with every accent the page can wear, on every surface the Poster layout stacks.

Decisions and things to know:

- **The Birthday look changed, on purpose, for AA.** On Golden hour the light-tone glass is now a smoked frost rather than a 10% white one, and secondary text is 80% white rather than 75% and 55%. That is what reading at AA on the brightest part of the gradient costs; it settles the Golden hour contrast note ticket 06 left for ticket 20. Secondary text never comes closer than 80% to full strength, so the page keeps a hierarchy and placeholders never look typed.
- **Other visible changes the rule required:** unchosen solid RSVP buttons are opaque accent (they were 85%); the chosen answer is ringed in the tone's text colour instead of the others fading to 60% (faded labels measured 3.2:1 on some combinations, and they are still buttons); the countdown is a solid accent chip (gold text on a gold wash could not reach AA over smoked glass); "Remove my RSVP" and the required-question asterisk use the accent ink; the guest list's "+N" uses faint text instead of 60% opacity.
- The dark tone on these dark backgrounds turns the cards into a milky frost over the dark page. It reads, and looks deliberate, but the maintainer's eye is still wanted on it, and on the smoked Golden hour card.
- The desktop panel overlaps the right edge of the content by about 40px at 1280px; ticket 11's side panel replaces it.
- The session lookup now runs on every event page, not only drafts, to know whether to show the Design button. A visitor without a session cookie costs nothing (Better Auth returns before the database).
