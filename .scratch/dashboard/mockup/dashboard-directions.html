What changes, and what I’d decide in the spec

The frame on every signed-in page

  • Header: the site’s name, New event, and your initials for the menu.
  • The tinted ground and poster glow reach Account, Site settings and an event’s guest pages too; their contents stay as they are for now.
  • The email verification notice moves under the header as a soft banner.

“Instance” leaves the interface

  • “Instance settings” becomes Site settings.
  • Host messages say the site’s name: “Orchard Lane is invitation only”, or “this site” when unnamed.
  • instance stays in the code and the self-hosting docs; CONTEXT.md records the rule.

States the dashboard must cover

  • No events yet: a single large “Make your first invitation” card on a gallery poster.
  • At the event limit: New event greys out, with the reason under it.
  • Uploaded posters and plain colour posters show as they do on the event page.
