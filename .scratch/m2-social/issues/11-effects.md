Decisions and things to know:
- Sparkles: 26 points from a small integer hash of the index (`Math.imul`), so the server and the browser draw the same ones. The prototype's formula (`(i*9301+49297)%233280`, and so on) puts every point on one diagonal across the top half of the screen, because each value is linear in `i`. Each point is 2 to 5 px with a glow of its own colour. One in three is in the accent, the rest white. The twinkle (opacity 0.05 to 0.9, scale 0.6 to 1.25) lasts 2.4 to 5 s with a negative delay, so the points start out of step and none jump at load.
- Doodles: seven shapes near the screen's edges (two rings, two dots, a rounded tile, a stroke, the prototype's squiggle) at opacity 0.3 to 0.5 in `text-theme-accent`. Each floats up 8 to 14 px with an 8-degree turn over 7 to 12 s. The float is on `transform`, so it composes with a shape's own `rotate`. On a phone most of them sit behind the glass and show only at the edges.
- Reduced motion: both layers are `motion-reduce:hidden` (display: none), and their animation only comes through `motion-safe:` classes. Their paces are custom properties (`--twinkle-duration`, `--float-duration` and so on) that mean nothing without the class, so `expectStill` sees zero durations. Confetti reads `matchMedia` through `useSyncExternalStore` (the server snapshot is the still one) and renders nothing. The sticker keeps its static `-rotate-6`.
- Confetti: 56 pieces in `var(--theme-accent)`, `var(--theme-text)` and `color-mix(in oklab, var(--theme-accent), white 50%)`. Each falls from 6 % above the top to 115 vh over 2.0 to 3.4 s, with up to 0.5 s of delay, sideways drift and up to a full turn, using `element.animate`. Sizes and paths are chosen in the effect, not in render. Once every fall's `finished` resolves, the layer is removed. It is portalled into the page's root (`useTheme().root`), so it wears the theme and stays fixed to the screen even inside the sheet (whose backdrop-filter would otherwise contain it). It is `z-70`, over the sheet (z-60) and the drawer (z-50), and `pointer-events-none`. While the sheet is open, Radix also turns pointer events off on `body`, so the browser test proves that taps go through on the Inline page.
- The confetti counter is bumped only when the theme's effect is Confetti at the moment of the save. A host who switches to Confetti afterwards gets no late fall.
- The preview card never sees the effect (`ResolvedTheme` carries no effect). A browser test compares the PNG bytes across all four effects.
- The background measurement script (`scripts/measure-backgrounds.ts`) uses a new event, which now sparkles. I checked whether the sparkles move its numbers, and they do not: with them in, Golden hour and Midnight measure the stored values to within rounding (its `driftTo` pauses the twinkle with the drift, and the 24 px glass blur flattens a 2-5 px point). So the script is unchanged.
