It stays open across the redraw that follows posting.
- **The script** (`src/themes/thread-script.ts`). `threadTurns(reply, event)` lists the turns the flow's steps ask, one thing at a time (name, email when asked; how many plus-ones, then each one's name when the host requires them; each question). `threadMessages({ step, at, reply }, event)` is the conversation for a position: the ask (or the cancelled or draft line when idle on a closed event), the guest's status in their words, every turn answered and the one they are on, and once saved the done bubble, followed by the closed line on an event that has since closed. Bubbles carry keys and either a line of `EventPage.thread` with values, a `Rsvp` word (yes, no, just me), literal text (a name, a prompt, an option), or a list of picks (joined by `format.list` in the browser). 15 unit tests per step and status.
- **The composer** (`src/app/e/[slug]/thread-composer.tsx`) is the flow's presentation for Thread: it renders the conversation (`role="log"`) under the host's bubbles and the sticky glass composer at the foot (`pb-[max(env(safe-area-inset-bottom),0.75rem)]`). It keeps a local turn index (`at`) within the hook's step, as the prototype's `qi`, reset to 0 when the step changes. Chips wear `cardButtonClasses(buttonStyle)`; a pressed chip (a pick, or the current single-choice answer) is filled with the accent and ringed in the text colour. Text turns are a pill input with Send laid over its end (Enter sends; Send is disabled while empty), with Skip for an optional turn. The last turn's answer is flushed into hidden fields (`flushSync`) and the form is submitted to `flow.send`, so `send` receives exactly the FormData ticket 12 describes. Single-choice chips go through `pickOption` and move on only when an answer stands, so tapping the current answer of an optional question while editing takes it back and stays (Skip or another chip moves on). After done: Change my answer, Edit my details, Remove my RSVP, exactly as the Poster offers them, on a closed event too.
- **Refusals.** A refusal tied to a field rewinds to that field's turn once the RSVP comes back (email: the email turn; plus-one names: the first name; others: the step's first turn), with the message in an alert above the composer. One about no field (too fast, closed) leaves every turn answered and offers "Send RSVP" again.
- **Pacing (ruled).** Everything the page holds when it opens is server-rendered and shown at once: the host's first six bubbles arrive 90 ms apart (`motion-safe:` animate-in with delays), the rest with the sixth, the card fading from a trace (`fade-in-1`) so it counts as the largest paint at once. A returning guest's whole conversation shows at once. After that the guest's bubbles show as they are said, and each host line follows 650 ms of typing dots (`animate-theme-typing`, a new keyframe in `globals.css`). The page scrolls so the newest line sits above the composer only when the conversation grows, never on load. Under reduced motion (`useReducedMotion`, now exported from `effects/confetti.tsx`) every line is there at once, no dots render, the scroll is instant, and nothing has a duration.
- **Focus.** When the guest answers, the focus waits on the composer (`tabIndex=-1`) and moves to the next turn's field, else its first enabled chip. A saved RSVP puts the focus on the done bubble's words; Change and Remove put it on the status chip, as `land` asks.
- **Legibility.** Every surface is one `legibility.ts` already solves, and `SURFACES` is unchanged. The header and composer are glass (card), their chips and field strong glass on it (inset), solid chips the accent. The host's bubbles are strong glass (strongGlass), and chips in them (Open in Maps, Copy edit link, the calendar) are only a ring, so their labels sit on the bubble's glass. The comments bubble is glass, because `CommentsSection`'s comment box is strong glass, which is solved on glass (inset) and not on strong glass. The guest's own bubbles, the initials, the countdown and pressed chips are filled with the accent in `on-accent`. The sent line sits on the veil. There is no accent ink anywhere in Thread. The layout paints no background of its own, so sparkles and doodles show between the bubbles.
- **Chips for the shared pieces.** `MapLink` takes `chip`, and `AddToCalendar` takes `chips` (returning three ring chips to sit in the done bubble); the page sets them under Thread.
