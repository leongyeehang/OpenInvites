Poster editor

A host makes the invitation by editing it directly, the way Apple Invites and Partiful work. It runs in your browser only; nothing is saved or sent.

Try it

  1. Change the poster. Tap Change poster, then try Gallery, Emoji and Colour, or upload a photo.
  2. Type the title on the invitation. Tap it and type.
  3. Fill in the details. Tap Add a place, or tap the date to change it.
  4. Style it. Title, Theme and Effect in the toolbar each change the invitation as you tap.
  5. Open Settings for capacity, approval, questions, contact details and emails.
  6. Publish, then reply as a guest.

What changes from 0.4

  • No more form at /events/new. A new event opens as a blank invitation and saves as you type.
  • The Design drawer becomes four buttons on the invitation: Poster, Title, Theme, Effect.
  • Templates fold into the poster gallery. Picking a poster also picks a title font, accent and effect that suit it.
  • Posters come from four places: the gallery, emoji patterns, colours, or an upload.
  • Guests get a poster-first page: the poster full width, the details in one list, the reply buttons pinned to the bottom.
  • Capacity, approval, questions, contact details, the guest list and emails move into one Settings sheet, with the same defaults as 0.4.
  • Where M4's features land, tagged M4: date polling in the date sheet, the photo album and comment reactions on the guest page.

What stays

The theme engine and its contrast checks, uploads and their processing, the effects, the reply steps (questions, email codes, waitlist, approval) and every M3 setting. Mostly the screens change, not the server.
