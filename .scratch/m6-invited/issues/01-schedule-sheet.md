## Notes for the implementer

- The glossary word is **Schedule** (see the spec's proposals); never "timeline", "agenda" or "programme" in code, messages or tests.
- The toolbar is a fixed `grid-cols-5` (`src/editor/settings-sheet.tsx`, `EditorToolbar`): do not add a sixth tool; the details row is the way in.
- `EditorSheet`'s `footer` slot sits outside the form (`sheet.tsx:83`); keeping the radios inside the form avoids a `form=` attribute on Done.
- Commit subject, e.g. `Schedule: the host lists the day's timed items`.
